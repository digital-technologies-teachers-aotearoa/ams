# Costs sheet

**Who this page is for:** client decision-makers — the committee members approving costs before your site is provisioned.

This page will list every recurring and one-off cost your organisation will incur: domain registration, transactional email, forum hosting, and website hosting, plus a note on your separate, pre-existing email costs.
