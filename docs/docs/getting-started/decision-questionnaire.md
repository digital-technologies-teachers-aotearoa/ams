# Decision questionnaire

**Who this page is for:** client decision-makers — the committee members approving costs and making decisions before your site is provisioned.

This page will be the single questionnaire your committee fills in once, covering your organisation name and domain, languages, membership model, notification preferences, optional features, hosting-region preferences, your email situation, branding assets, and who holds each named role.
